# FK-UZ-2 1099-modda: Yuridik shaxsning firma nomidan tovar belgisida foydalanish

**Rasmiy Lex.uz havolasi:** [https://lex.uz/docs/-180552#-198424](https://lex.uz/docs/-180552#-198424)
**Bog'liq havolalar:** Mavjud emas

## Norma matni:
Yuridik shaxsning firma nomidan unga tegishli tovar belgisida foydalanilishi mumkin.
